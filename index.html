How to Install on iOS (iPhone / iPad)

To use Fitness Pro like a native app with full screen and offline capability on iOS Safari:

  1. Open this web app in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap the Share button [ □↗ ] at the bottom (or top) toolbar in Safari.
  3. Scroll down the menu and tap "Add to Home Screen" (افزودن به صفحه اصلی).
  4. Tap Add in the top right corner. The Fitness Pro icon will now appear on your home screen!
